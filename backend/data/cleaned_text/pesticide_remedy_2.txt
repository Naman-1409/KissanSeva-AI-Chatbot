Crop: Tomato
Disease Or Pest: Early Blight
Agent: Alternaria solani
Pathogen Type: Fungus
Recommended Treatment: Spray Difolatan 0.2% or Dithane M 45 0.2% or Bavistin 0.1%
Treatment Type: Chemical
Active Ingredient: Difolatan, Dithane M 45, Bavistin
Dosage: 0.2% or 0.1%
Application Interval Days: 10
Stage Applicable: Vegetative/Fruiting
Organic Option: Use crop rotation and remove affected leaves
Precautions: Avoid overuse of chemical fungicides

Crop: Rice
Disease Or Pest: Leaf Blast
Agent: Pyricularia oryzae
Pathogen Type: Fungus
Recommended Treatment: Apply Tricyclazole 75 WP at 300 g/ha and remove volunteer plants
Treatment Type: Chemical
Active Ingredient: Tricyclazole
Dosage: 300 g/ha
Application Interval Days: 14
Stage Applicable: Panicle initiation
Organic Option: Use resistant varieties, maintain proper water depth
Precautions: Avoid excessive nitrogen use

Crop: Maize
Disease Or Pest: Stem Borer
Agent: Busseola fusca
Pathogen Type: Insect
Recommended Treatment: Install pheromone traps, spray Spinosad 45 SC 0.3 L/ha when >10% damage
Treatment Type: Chemical/Insecticidal
Active Ingredient: Spinosad
Dosage: 0.3 L/ha
Application Interval Days: 10
Stage Applicable: Early vegetative
Organic Option: Use natural enemies, avoid broad spectrum insecticides
Precautions: Avoid spraying during flowering

Crop: Cotton
Disease Or Pest: Bollworm
Agent: Helicoverpa armigera
Pathogen Type: Insect
Recommended Treatment: Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG at 220 g/ha or Bt formulation
Treatment Type: Chemical/Biological
Active Ingredient: Emamectin Benzoate, Bacillus thuringiensis
Dosage: 220 g/ha
Application Interval Days: 15
Stage Applicable: Flowering/Boll formation
Organic Option: Use pheromone traps and neem based sprays
Precautions: Rotate insecticides to prevent resistance

Crop: Potato
Disease Or Pest: Late Blight
Agent: Phytophthora infestans
Pathogen Type: Fungus
Recommended Treatment: Spray Mancozeb 0.25% or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP
Treatment Type: Chemical
Active Ingredient: Mancozeb, Metalaxyl
Dosage: 0.25% or 2.5 g/L
Application Interval Days: 10
Stage Applicable: Tuber formation
Organic Option: Remove infected leaves, ensure proper spacing
Precautions: Do not spray near harvest
